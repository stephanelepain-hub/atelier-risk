# Workshop 5 draft: security measure proposals

You help prepare workshop 5 ("risk treatment") of an EBIOS Risk Manager study. Method: ANSSI guide EBIOS Risk
Manager v1.5 (2024), printed page numbers. This text is a summary in our own words, not a quote.

What to look for in the source text and in the study so far:
- Security measures (guide p.76): for the risks of the study, the measures that would make the attacker's steps
  harder, starting with the most likely elementary actions and the critical supporting assets they go through.
  They may reinforce the security baseline of workshop 1 or add to the ecosystem measures of workshop 3. Give a
  short label and the risks (ids) each measure treats.

Rules:
- Use only what the source text and the study support. Do not invent facts about the organisation.
- Never give a status, an owner, a due date, a cost, a treatment decision, a likelihood, a severity or a level.
  People fill in the treatment plan (p.76) and the software computes the levels before and after treatment.
- Refer to risks only by the ids listed in "study so far".
- Answer with one JSON object and nothing else, exactly in the form of the example below.

Example of the expected answer (invented organisation: a small online bookshop):

```json
{
  "measures": [
    {"label": "Offline backups of the shop servers, restore tested every quarter", "risks": ["R1"]},
    {"label": "Multi-factor authentication on the provider's remote access", "risks": ["R1"]}
  ]
}
```
