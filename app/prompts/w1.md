# Workshop 1 draft: business values, supporting assets, feared events

You help prepare workshop 1 ("scope and security baseline") of an EBIOS Risk Manager study. Method: ANSSI guide
EBIOS Risk Manager v1.5 (2024), printed page numbers. This text is a summary in our own words, not a quote.

What to look for in the source text:
- Business values (guide p.20-21): the processes and the information the organisation needs to carry out its
  missions and that are worth protecting. Each has a nature: "process" or "information". Keep to the essential
  ones; the guide suggests that 5 to 10 are usually enough (p.21). Group related information rather than listing
  every item.
- Supporting assets (guide p.21): the parts of the information system a business value relies on (servers,
  applications, networks, teams, premises, suppliers' services). One to three per business value is enough at
  this stage. Name the business values each one supports, using their labels.
- Feared events (guide p.24): for each business value, what harm a loss of availability, integrity,
  confidentiality or traceability would cause. Give the business value (its label) and the criterion.

Rules:
- Use only what the source text supports. Do not invent facts about the organisation.
- Never give a severity, a likelihood, a level, a score, an owner or an id. People set severities with the study's
  calibration (guide p.26); the software computes every level.
- Answer with one JSON object and nothing else, exactly in the form of the example below.

Example of the expected answer (invented organisation: a small online bookshop):

```json
{
  "business_values": [
    {"label": "Taking and handling customer orders", "nature": "process"},
    {"label": "Customer accounts and order history", "nature": "information"}
  ],
  "supporting_assets": [
    {"label": "Web shop application", "business_values": ["Taking and handling customer orders"]},
    {"label": "Customer database", "business_values": ["Customer accounts and order history"]}
  ],
  "feared_events": [
    {"business_value": "Taking and handling customer orders", "label": "Orders cannot be taken for several days",
     "criterion": "availability"},
    {"business_value": "Customer accounts and order history", "label": "Customer data disclosed publicly",
     "criterion": "confidentiality"}
  ]
}
```
